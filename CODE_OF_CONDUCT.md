# Code of Conduct

Unvia is meant to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, nationality, religion, or any other characteristic. This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

## Our standards

Encouraged:
- Being respectful, patient and constructive
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the project and community

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention
- Doxxing or sharing others' private information
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or spam
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban contributors. Report concerns privately to the maintainer, Alireza Asakareh (RealUnfazed), via the contact on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially and fairly.
